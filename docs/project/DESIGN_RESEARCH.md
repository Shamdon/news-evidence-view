# Reader design research dossier

Research date: 2026-10-13. This informs reversible prototypes, not an established consumer outcome. All prototype report text and numerical evidence are synthetic. Live service work is separate. No claim of market demand, misinformation resistance or commercial clearance follows from this dossier.

## Questions and implications

| Source and evidence class | Finding and limits | Concrete design implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Lateral reading and monetary incentives to spot disinformation about science](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35383208/), 2022-04-05, empirical: two preregistered experiments, N=5387 | Both interventions increased fact-checking strategies. The advice pop-up improved accuracy when source was unknown; incentives had broader effects. A simulated feed is not this service, and a prompt alone did not uniformly improve accuracy. | Make original source and evidence family reachable beside a claim. Avoid counting copied reports. Test whether readers actually inspect provenance rather than assuming a reminder works. |
| [Communicating uncertainty about facts, numbers and science](https://doi.org/10.1098/RSOS.181870), 2019, research review | Responses to epistemic uncertainty depend on format and context; the evidence is heterogeneous. Uncertainty disclosure is not a guarantee of trust or comprehension. | State the specific missing observation, date or scope alongside the claim. Do not invent numerical confidence scores. Test interpretation of “unresolved” against “false”. |
| [Effects of communicating uncertainty on public trust in facts and numbers](https://api.repository.cam.ac.uk/server/api/core/bitstreams/a8e48782-0290-437d-90a8-2de8b5d2dc08/content), 2020, primary empirical paper | Communicated uncertainty was perceived; trust changes were small and mainly associated with verbal uncertainty in the studied contexts. This does not validate arbitrary badge vocabularies or allegations. | Show what changes and what remains unknown, with original numerical values when applicable. Use specific limits rather than a blanket “low confidence” verdict. |
| [W3C Making Content Usable](https://www.w3.org/TR/coga-usable/), 2021-04-29, authoritative guidance rather than controlled outcome evidence | Clear page purpose, conventional controls, clear steps and short paths help orientation. Guidance is broader than news assessment. | Plain-language submission, three-step guided alternative, persistent fixture label, named actions, explicit recoverable failure. Avoid technical queries and icon-only controls. |
| [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/), 2023 recommendation, accessibility requirements | Reflow, keyboard access, visible focus, names and minimum target size are relevant. Passing automated checks alone does not establish accessibility. | Native buttons/selects/dialog, 44px control target, single-column mobile layouts, skip link, semantic table headers and scroll contained to matrix. Manual keyboard and mobile evaluation remain required. |
| [Full Fact: how we fact check](https://fullfact.org/about/how-we-fact-check/), official workflow, accessed 2026-10-13 | Describes checking source material and presenting reasoning. This is an organization's account of its process, not empirical proof of reader benefit. Search extract available; full-page fetch failed during research. | Original wording, inspectable reasoning and explicit evidence limits; do not copy publisher branding or imply automated work has its editorial review. |
| [Google Fact Check Tools API](https://developers.google.com/fact-check/tools/api), official product documentation, accessed 2026-10-13 | Searches fact-checked claims and provides claim-review interfaces. An existing review is separate from original evidence and can have its own framing or date. | Headline discovery must show candidate reports; a selected title is not itself a verified claim. Preserve review/source/evidence relationships and dates rather than a global verdict. |
| [USGS magnitude updates](https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/whywhen-does-usgs-update-magnitude-earthquake), official evidence workflow, accessed 2026-10-13 | Estimates change as data and analysis become available. One event with revisions is not independent corroboration. | Evidence journey separates event, initial release, revision and snapshot times. A map location estimate must not imply building-level causation. |
| [DocumentCloud document viewer](https://github.com/documentcloud/document-viewer/blob/master/README.rdoc), original product code documentation, accessed 2026-10-13 | Supports document views and annotations tied to pages/spans. It is a specialist tool; its structure need not suit a quick feed reader. | Annotated reader direction ties exact wording to a claim inspector; compare reorientation cost on mobile rather than assuming side-by-side is best. |
| [Gapminder Dollar Street about](https://www.gapminder.org/dollar-street/about?lng=en), official product explanation, accessed 2026-10-13 | Uses specific household observations for comparisons across places/income. It does not make selected examples representative of an entire country. | Comparison direction exposes unit/coverage limits. No documentary photos are fabricated; the population fixture explicitly prevents national-to-every-town inference. |

## Design judgments and competing hypotheses

- Quick-context users may benefit from an initial plain-language summary and one clear next action; deeper users may prefer the matrix. This is a hypothesis, not a finding.
- A document-first interface keeps wording central but may trap readers in a compelling narrative. Direct evidence and independent-source navigation must counter that risk.
- Chronology is valuable for estimate changes and relative dates, but can suggest a causal chain. The journey explicitly denies that inference and keeps missing times visible.
- Geographic context is useful only when the claim has a resolved place. Unresolved Springfield gets no pin. Country orientation is separate from reported event location; position does not support involvement. No globe dominates the population or allegation account.
- A guided sequence reduces visible complexity but introduces extra transitions and can hide claims. Persistent named steps and a reversible path expose that tradeoff.
- Theme choice is independent from layout, evidence or capability. Night and paper tokens do not alter saved fixture values or interpretations.

## Gaps

English prototypes do not establish multilingual usability. Content expansion, localized date handling, bidirectional text, translation provenance and native-speaker testing remain necessary before language claims. No real participants were recruited. No experimental reader-benefit or commercial outcome is established. There was no direct logged-in observation of the cited products; their documented workflows are identified as documentation, not observed UX behavior.
