# Product brief: consumer report investigation

## Purpose and audience

Consumer web app for source criticism, source evaluation and information evaluation. Ordinary internet users submit a URL, headline, text or supported media and examine what it reports, where/when it happened, supporting/challenging sources and unresolved questions.

Working description: “Check the news behind your feed. Submit a link, headline or report and explore its claims alongside relevant sources, local observations and context.” This describes intent, not a claim of universal working input/source support.

## Primary journey

Submit → acquire or discover candidate reporting → identify inspectable factual claims → resolve useful context → choose relevant capabilities/sources → retrieve evidence → understandable overview → inspect original wording, evidence, contradictions and gaps → save/revisit the exact recorded state.

A headline is a discovery request, not a factual report. Candidate reporting remains explicit; do not silently choose an unrelated story. Targeted ambiguity questions are acceptable; making readers configure an investigation is not.

The reader chooses what to inspect, not subject checkers, providers, coordinates or date-window parameters. Progressive disclosure keeps source detail and uncertainty available. No global true/fake verdict.

## General architecture

Provider-neutral evidence/domain records and independent acquisition, interpretation, resolution, retrieval, orchestration, persistence, presentation/theme and geographic capabilities. Typed source adapters preserve metadata meaningful to their source. A general investigation selects adapters according to claims; examples such as seismic events and population reports demonstrate replaceability without defining permanent identity/schema.

Source relevance and independence are not inferred merely from separate URLs. Retrieve original reporting/documents, authoritative records, other reporting and geographic/historical/local observations where implemented and relevant. Local media feeds need explicit coverage/acquisition-time controls before use. A present-day live feed is not evidence of a historical account. Insufficient or unsupported evidence is legitimate.

## Design exploration

Research and compare at least four materially different functioning alternatives on the same representative reports and adversarial/insufficient cases. Preserve alternatives and evaluation evidence. Choose a reversible recommendation after comparison, keeping the aligned real-input journey runnable separately from explicit fixture prototypes.

## Evidence, rights and scope

Untrusted source material cannot execute instructions or expand provider access. Preserve source spans/timecodes when supported, correction history, source families and publication/observation/event/retrieval times. Unknowns remain unknown; source dependence and meaningful differences remain visible. Never present source assertions or reconstructed movement as independently observed facts.

Audiovisual breadth is an ambition. Implemented inputs/sources, acquisition restrictions and unassessed claims must be described accurately. Preserve license/notices; no commercial clearance, human reader benefit, misinformation resistance or commercial demand is asserted.

## Acceptance correction

Tests and browser journeys must verify consumer input without technical configuration; real article/report acquisition and evidence; headline candidate selection; materially different reporting subjects through shared contracts; saved snapshots; architecture/theme/provider/layer/reduced-composition/lifecycle gates; and four functioning designs evaluated with actionable research. Documentation alone does not satisfy this brief.
