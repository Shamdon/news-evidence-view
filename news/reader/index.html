What are you bringing?

The local service reads public HTML; no sign-in or access restrictions are bypassed. Headlines search a public news index and require you to choose a candidate. Audio/video/PDF are not acquired; paste a permitted transcript or excerpt instead.

What gets sent, and what can currently be examined?

Submitted links are requested from their publisher by the local service. Headline words go to a public news index. Report text is interpreted locally; only derived evidence lookup parameters reach record providers. No automatic publication or server-side account storage. Explicit browser saves and exports contain text and evidence and are not encrypted.

Factual candidates use conservative English and limited Swedish interpretation. Configured record capabilities currently cover seismic events and annual country population. Other statements remain unassessed, with original wording preserved. Local media feeds and audiovisual transcription are not yet configured. Source family, time, coverage and missing evidence remain inspectable.
