# Goal and acceptance definitions

## Definition of Done: initial product slice

- A location-specific article enters through a documented supported input.
- Claims link to source spans and retain correction history.
- Ambiguous places/dates remain explicit.
- Evidence retains provenance and known publication/observation/retrieval times.
- Map, timeline and evidence panel separate claims, observations and inferences.
- Supporting, conflicting, missing and dependent-source evidence can be inspected.
- Saved accounts reopen with their recorded evidence state; missing content is explicit.
- Functional, security, accessibility and regression checks pass for implemented scope.
- The interface accurately identifies implemented and unsupported behavior.

## Definition of Failure

Invented sources/events/locations presented as evidence; claims silently promoted to map facts; publication time mistaken for observation time; duplicate reporting counted as corroboration; absence of evidence treated as proof of absence; saved evidence silently replaced; claimed improvements unsupported by evaluation.

## Definition of Success

Predefine an evaluation and scoring rubric comparing the service with source-linked reading. Measure correct place/date interpretation, recognition of unresolved claims, ability to locate conflicting/supporting evidence and unwarranted confidence. Set acceptance thresholds before gathering results.

Commercial success separately requires evidence of recurring need, willingness to pay and sustainable delivery costs. No outcomes or numerical targets are asserted at setup.

Repository preparation does not meet product Definition of Done.
